# Interpretation and power

This page applies Stuart Hall’s encoding/decoding model to show how meaning is negotiated. Senders may encode preferred meanings, but audiences can accept, negotiate, or oppose them.

This lens is useful when communication problems involve institutions, platforms, and unequal influence. It highlights who has power to frame events and whose readings get treated as legitimate.
