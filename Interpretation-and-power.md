# Interpretation and Power

Interpretation and power can be seen in many ways. Communication is made up of linked moments with unique meanings and AI can disrupt that. 

An employer when uploading a job listing on a platform makes it have a unique meaning; just like the candidate makes his/her resume with a specialized meaning. This could be where interpretation between these two could have different meanings and if there is artificial intelligence checking out the candidate’s resume, the messages would not match. 

This suggests there are meanings embedded in a message, but the meanings intended are not always the same and AI can disrupt the intended meaning of these job sites and resumes.
