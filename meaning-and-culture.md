# Meaning and culture

This page frames the same communication problem through semiotics. Instead of asking only whether a message moved efficiently, it asks how signs create meaning within social and cultural systems.

Here you can map symbols, narratives, and recurring patterns that shape interpretation. This helps explain why the “same” message can lead to different understandings across groups.
