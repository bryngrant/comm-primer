# Meaning and culture

This page frames the way that the meaning and culture, semiotics, is shown in AI impacting career opportunities. 

Through semiotics, we understand more about the impact that AI has on not just the career options available to individuals in the online world but how they interpret them as well. Semiotics is directly related to meaning and culture since people interpret the words and signs in a certain way depending on their background, experience, and belief.

In semiotics, it can be explained how the role of AI is not limited to simply showing people their career opportunities but also shapes the way in which people understand the significance of their chosen careers.
