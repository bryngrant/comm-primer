# Semiotics

This page introduces Semiotics and how it is a study that shows how people develop meaning using words and symbols. 

In my the job market, words such as “experience,” “entry-level,” and “competitive” will help a person get a feel of the nature of the job just by looking at a few words. 

The AI uses such words in job postings in order to attract the attention of people and make them click on the job listing.

The key question is "what does the message mean?" and "how did I interpret it?" This helps really understand and figure out the right message.
