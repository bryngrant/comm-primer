# Communication Theory for Everyday Problems in the AI Century

Welcome. This site introduces major schools of communication theory for readers who may not be communication specialists but regularly face communication breakdowns at home, at work, and online.

## What this project is for

This project aims to make the commonplace visible. It uses classic and contemporary communication frameworks to help explain why messages fail, how meanings shift, and what practical decisions people can make when communication problems involve artificial intelligence.

## What you will find here

- A guided tour of six perspectives and practical pages.
- Plain-language framing designed for broad audiences.
- Space for examples, case studies, and images you will add.

Use the navigation to move through each lens and build your own analysis.
